## ✨ What You'll Get

When you ask for an outfit, the AI will:

### 👔 Complete Outfit Bundle:
- **Top**: Shirt, t-shirt, or dress
- **Bottom**: Pants, jeans, or skirt (if needed)
- **Footwear**: Matching shoes or sneakers
- **Optional**: Jacket or blazer for formal occasions

### 🎨 Smart Features:
- ✅ **Color-coordinated** pieces
- ✅ **Occasion-appropriate** styles
- ✅ **Budget-friendly** options (if specified)
- ✅ **In-stock** items only
- ✅ **Styling tips** included

### 💰 Price Information:
- Individual item prices
- **Total outfit price**
- Easy to budget

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### "No outfit found"
- Inventory might lack matching items
- Try a different occasion
- Check product stock levels

### Budget Issues
- Requested budget might be too low
- Try increasing budget or removing constraint

### Works Perfectly When:
- ✅ Diverse product inventory
- ✅ Good stock levels
- ✅ Products have proper color/category data
- ✅ Budget is reasonable
